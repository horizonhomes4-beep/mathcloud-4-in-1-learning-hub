Guest help centre

Frequently asked questions

Answers to common questions about the MathCloud Tutorial hub and how to get started.

What is MathCloud Tutorial?

MathCloud Tutorial provides digital learning activities and tutoring enquiries designed to help learners build literacy and mathematics skills. This page is the public information hub for guests.

Which learning programmes are available?

The hub links to Reading Fluency, Spelling & Vocabulary, Reading Comprehension and Maths Quiz. Each programme has its own app page and its own learning flow.

Are all four learning apps the same?

No. Each app focuses on a different skill and opens in its own folder. The public hub does not replace or merge their individual index pages, login experiences or learning logic.

How do I start using a learning app?

Choose a programme card above and open it. Follow the instructions displayed inside that specific app. Some features may require a passcode, school access or an active subscription.

Is there a free option?

Availability of a demo or free activity depends on the individual programme. Open the app and check its on-screen instructions before subscribing.

How much does a subscription cost?

The subscription page linked from this hub describes the current enrolment options for the four programmes. Review the terms there and confirm the applicable fee before making payment. Do not assume every programme or tutoring session has the same price.

How do I subscribe or enrol a student?

Open “Explore subscriptions” or “View subscription options”, complete the details requested on that page and follow its enrolment instructions. Contact the team if you need help selecting programmes.

Can a parent or school enrol multiple learners?

The subscription/enrolment page is the best place to review available learner and programme options. For school-wide arrangements, contact MathCloud Tutorial directly to discuss the requirements.

Does a subscription automatically unlock every app?

Not necessarily. Access depends on the programme and the enrolment or access arrangements attached to it. Check the specific app and confirm with the team which programmes are included.

What is one-to-one online tutoring?

It is personalised online support where a tutor works with one learner on agreed subjects or goals. Contact MathCloud Tutorial to discuss the subject, class level, schedule, availability and fee before booking.

Which subjects can I request for tutoring?

Send the learner's class, subject and learning goal through WhatsApp. The team can confirm which tutoring support is available and whether it fits the learner's needs.

How do online tutoring sessions work?

Session arrangements, platform, duration, schedule and fees should be agreed directly with the team before the session is confirmed.

Can learners use the hub on a phone?

The pages are designed to adapt to mobile, tablet and desktop screen sizes. A stable internet connection may be needed for online app features.

Do I need to install an app?

These programmes open in a web browser. If an individual app offers installation instructions, follow those instructions on that app's own page.

What if I forget a passcode or cannot sign in?

Read the sign-in instructions shown by the individual app or ask your teacher/school for the correct access details. For programme-specific issues, contact MathCloud Tutorial and include the app name and a description of the error. Never send your password publicly.

Can I access an admin dashboard from this page?

No. This is a guest-facing information page. It intentionally contains no links to administrative files, dashboards, seeders or management tools.

Does this page change my app data or Firebase rules?

No. This landing page is separate from the learning apps and does not initialise Firebase, change database rules, or access admin functionality.

How does the newsletter subscription work?

Enter your email and press Subscribe. WhatsApp opens with a ready-to-send request containing your email address. You must send the message yourself; this static page does not automatically save email addresses or send newsletters. Automated mailing requires a connected newsletter service.

How do I contact MathCloud Tutorial?

Use the WhatsApp contact button on this page. Send your question, the relevant programme name and enough detail for the team to assist you.

Who leads MathCloud Tutorial?

Marshall Jacob is presented here as the CEO of MathCloud Tutorial.

How can I help a learner get the most from the programmes?

Encourage short, regular practice sessions. Ask the learner to explain their reasoning, revisit missed questions and choose a clear next goal rather than focusing only on scores.

What should I do if a link does not open?

Make sure the project folder structure remains intact after deployment, including the four learning-app folders and their files. Use a static-hosting setup that serves the root index.html and preserves folder paths. If the problem continues, contact support with the link and screenshot of the error.
